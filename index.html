MCA Admission

2-Year Master of Computer Applications focusing on Full-Stack Systems, Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Architectures, and Cyber Defense.

  • Eligibility: BCA / B.Sc CS or degree with Maths (50% marks)
  • Quotas: LBS Govt Allotment (50%) & Management Merit (50%)
  • Placements: 100% placement track record with top IT leaders

5-Year Integrated Degree Direct After 12th

iMCA Admission

Integrated Master of Computer Applications directly after Plus Two (Class 12). Saves one academic year and leads directly to a Master's degree.

  • Eligibility: 10+2 with Maths / Stats / Computer Studies (45%)
  • Advantage: Dual-qualification pathway saving 1 year
  • Internships: Live software industry projects from 3rd year
