Institutional Legacy

The Institution

Over two decades of shaping world-class technocrats, leaders, and innovators.

The Federal Institute of Science and Technology (FISAT) is a self-financing private engineering college established and promoted by the Federal Bank Officers' Association Educational Society (FBOAES). FBOAES is an initiative of the Federal Bank Officers' Association (FBOA), the sole representative body of all Federal Bank officers.

FISAT occupies a unique position in South India's professional education sector. Established in 2002 with the motto "Focus on Excellence", the institution has been designed and developed as a Centre of Excellence in professional education. Over the years, FISAT has carved a niche for itself through its students' outstanding achievements in academics, placements, research, innovation, and co-curricular and extra-curricular activities.

In recognition of its academic excellence and robust governance, the University Grants Commission (UGC) conferred Autonomous Status on FISAT for a period of 10 years in 2025. The institution is accredited with an 'A+' Grade by NAAC with an impressive score of 3.45 CGPA in the 2nd cycle, and its core B.Tech programs are accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA).

Guiding Principles

Vision, Mission & Motto

Our Motto: "Focus on Excellence"

Our Vision

To evolve into a world-class professional institute committed to excellence in education, fostering holistic development, and empowering socially responsible global technocrats to drive sustainable growth through leadership in industry, innovation, research, and community engagement.

Our Mission

1. Academic Excellence & Innovation: To deliver high-quality professional education that promotes academic excellence, fosters the right attitude, cultivates essential skills, and encourages innovation and global competence through an industry-aligned curriculum and advanced research.

2. Ethical Leadership & Sustainability: To nurture socially responsible technocrats, impactful leaders, and accomplished management professionals, by promoting holistic growth, upholding ethical values, and championing sustainable practices for the advancement of both industry and society.

The Visionary Behind FISAT

Founder Chairman: Late Er. P. V. Mathew

The Federal Institute of Science And Technology owes its inception to the indomitable courage and philanthropic vision of Late Er. P. V. Mathew, former President of FBOAES. His golden motto — "Focus on Excellence" — continues to inspire every milestone at FISAT.
