UGC Autonomous Status

Autonomous Institution — 10-Year Recognition (2025–2035)

The University Grants Commission (UGC), New Delhi granted FISAT full Autonomous Status for 10 years, effective 2025. This recognition allows FISAT to design its own syllabus, conduct examinations, and award degrees under the affiliation of APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU), Thiruvananthapuram.

Autonomous status also recognises FISAT's consistent track record of academic excellence, research output, and governance quality — culminating from its prior NAAC A+ (3.45 CGPA) accreditation. Students graduating after 2025 receive degree certificates that explicitly bear the Autonomous designation.

Accreditation Journey

2002

Established — AICTE & Mahatma Gandhi University Affiliation

FISAT was founded by the Federal Bank Officers' Association Educational Society (FBOAES) and received AICTE approval for its first B.Tech programs.

2012

Affiliated to APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU)

Upon establishment of KTU, FISAT became one of its premier affiliated institutions, maintaining consistent top-rank placements in the university results.

2017–2020

NBA Accreditation — All Six B.Tech Programs

After completing the Outcome-Based Education (OBE) framework, all six B.Tech programs (CSE, ECE, EEE, ME, CE, EIE) earned NBA accreditation — a significant benchmark for engineering quality.

2021

NAAC Cycle 2 — 'A+' Grade, CGPA 3.45/4.0

NAAC's Peer Team assessed FISAT across seven criteria — Curricular Aspects, Teaching-Learning, Research, Infrastructure, Student Support, Governance, and Institutional Values — awarding the prestigious A+ grade.

2025

UGC Autonomous Status — 10-Year Recognition

UGC and KTU conferred Autonomous Institution status for 10 years (2025–2035), empowering FISAT to set its own curriculum, question papers, and evaluation processes.
